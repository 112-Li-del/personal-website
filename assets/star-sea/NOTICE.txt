Star Sea Express — third-party provenance
=========================================

1. Fragment shader (star-sea.js, FRAGMENT_SHADER)
   Original work: "Up in the Cloud Sea" / "Traveling in the Cloud" (2024)
   by Tianxiu (Tyson) Zhou, Texas A&M University, CSCE 646 Project 1.
     http://people.tamu.edu/~choutianxius/csce646/pr01/index.html
     https://github.com/choutianxius/csce646-digital-image
     shader source: .../pr01/main-image-source.txt
   It is itself adapted from mdb's ShaderToy "Traveling in the Cloud":
     https://www.shadertoy.com/view/Ndc3zl

   Retained from the original: the layered fbm cloud bands, the
   vector-to-raster train / bridge / wheels / smoke geometry, the
   foreground motion-blur pass, the vignette correction and the
   time-driven drift.
   Added for this site: a luminance-based navy/steel-blue regrade
   (nightGrade), a twinkling starfield and milky band (skyColor /
   starLayer), a uReveal uniform for scroll-driven depth reveals,
   window lights on the train, and GLSL safety fixes (pow() on
   possibly-negative bases replaced with explicit squares).
   ShaderToy code is publicly posted for reuse; this adaptation is used
   in a non-commercial personal portfolio. Credit is kept here and in
   the source file header.

2. Blue-noise texture (blue-noise.bin)
   1024x1024 R8, 1,048,576 bytes. Extracted from the red channel of the
   original blue_noise.png (1024x1024 RGBA, 4,202,841 bytes) in the
   GitHub repo: public/pr01/blue_noise.png, blob fbe0757e. The shader
   samples .x to build the fbm clouds, exactly as the original does.
   Shipping raw R8 bytes instead of the PNG cuts the payload from
   4.2 MB to 1 MB and avoids browser image-decode failures.

3. Nothing here is served from a third-party CDN at runtime; both the
   shader and the texture are local files.
