The original character artwork and animation are played with Rive. This site crops transparent artboard padding, positions the character in the page, and adds a waist tether plus a moving cover. It does not claim the character or original animation as original work. Visible attribution is in the site footer.

Rive Canvas runtime: @rive-app/canvas 2.40.1 (MIT), self-hosted JS and WASM. License: RUNTIME-LICENSE.txt.

Head-overlay test: the original circular head is masked during canvas composition and replaced with a transparent manga profile edited from the user's supplied portrait. Its position and angle follow the original head bone. Original body artwork and motion remain from JcToon; the supplied .riv file itself is unchanged. Portrait processing and prompt: ../profile-head-source.md.
